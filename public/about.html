Unsaid by Heart is a full-stack poetry platform where emotions are turned into words and shared through a simple, expressive interface.

I write here as unsaid.by_heart — a place for the unsaid, where fleeting thoughts and quiet feelings gather. A space where emotions that often go unspoken are gently held in words — memories, silence, love, and everything in between. Step in, read slowly, and linger where the words choose to stay.
